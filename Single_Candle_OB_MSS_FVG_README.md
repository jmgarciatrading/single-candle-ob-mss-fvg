# Single Candle OB + MSS + FVG

`Single_Candle_OB_MSS_FVG.pine` is a TradingView Pine Script v6 overlay. It marks the candle that closes through a swing after the next candle leaves that swing inside a body fair value gap. It is not a strategy and it does not place orders.

Paste the script into TradingView. This workspace does not compile Pine. If the indicator is already on the chart, set **Swing Length** to 1. TradingView keeps the previous input, and the old default of 3 misses the one-candle swing on the examples below.

## The candle

The highlighted candle is the one that closes through the swing. It closes up through a swing high, or down through a swing low. A wick that tags the swing and closes back does not qualify.

The box is not drawn when that candle closes. The next candle has to leave the broken swing strictly inside the body fair value gap. A bullish gap needs the previous candle's body high below the swing and the confirming candle's body low above it. A bearish gap needs the previous candle's body low above the swing and the confirming candle's body high below it. The confirming candle can be either color. A wick may overlap that gap. A confirming candle that leaves the swing on a body edge, or outside the gap, leaves no mark. The swing is still used once the break candle qualifies, including when the next candle does not leave it inside the gap.

A later candle that closes through the marked candle in the opposite direction is marked on that close. A bearish mark is closed by an up close above its high. A bullish mark is closed by a down close below its low. That candle does not have to pass the fair value gap. It does have to pass the time window. Min RR still applies. Below the multiple, or outside the window, the old mark is removed, nothing is drawn, and the direction is booked. The strategy does not send an order for an outside flip. The next close through that flip candle can flip again. A wick through the marked candle leaves it in place.

Each swing is used once. `Swing Breach Lookback` is how long an unclaimed close-through stays open before it is booked. The default is 2, so a close-through outside the time window is booked two candles later. 0 books it on the break candle. A larger number waits until that window ends. A close-through inside the time window is booked on the break candle. A wick that never closes through does not book the swing.

The first break of a swing, and any later break against the previous break, is MSS. A later break in the same direction is BOS. The kind is stored with the formation and is not drawn.

`Swing Length` is the pivot size for `ta.pivothigh()` and `ta.pivotlow()`. The default is 1, the nearest one-candle swing. A pivot is available only after its right-side bars have closed. Raise the length to require a larger swing.

## Chart

The drawing is a box on the break candle only, from that candle's high to its low, and a dotted line at the midpoint of that range. The box stays on that one candle. The gap and the swing stay in the detection and are not drawn. With `Limit at the swing`, the stop, the entry, and the target extend together until a later candle trades the entry price. That touch stops the lines on that candle. `Market at close` draws those lines on the signal and leaves them there. A candle that trades the stop hides the box, the midpoint, the stop, the entry, and the target. That includes a stop traded after the entry.

## Target

`Timeframe Target` defaults to 1H. `Daily` uses the symbol's daily candle. `Limit at the swing` puts the stop on the previous candle's low when the signal is bullish, and on the previous candle's high when it is bearish. That distance is measured from the breached swing. `Market at close` puts the stop on the open of the displacement candle and measures the distance from that candle's close. A bullish target is the newest confirmed swing high above that entry on the selected timeframe. A bearish target is the newest swing low below it. `Target Swing Length` defaults to 6. `Max Target` defaults to 14. A neighbor equal to the pivot does not qualify. When no swing is beyond the entry, the target is two times that stop distance. `Max Target` caps a farther swing. It does not cap the two-times fallback.

`Off` does not use a swing. With `Max Target` above 0, `Off` draws that multiple of the same distance. `Max Target` at 0 with `Off` leaves the target off. The stop still draws when the candle is marked. A target closer to the entry than the stop is left off. Equal to that distance still draws. `Min RR` defaults to 2. With `Min RR` at 0, the displacement candle still highlights. `Min RR` above 0 leaves the candle unmarked when the final target is shorter than the multiple. The final target is the higher-timeframe swing, the two-times fallback when no swing is beyond the entry, or the `Max Target` cap when that cap is closer. `Limit at the swing` measures a bullish entry up from the breached swing and a bearish entry down from it. `Market at close` measures the same target from the close of the displacement candle, with the stop on the open of that same candle. Equal to the multiple still marks. The break is still booked. A missing swing uses the two-times fallback, and that fallback is unmarked when two is below `Min RR`. With the target off, `Min RR` above 0 leaves the candle unmarked. `Max Target` defaults to 14. Above 0 it caps a farther swing at that multiple, so a swing beyond 14R is drawn at 14R.

The red stop, the black entry, and the green target share one right edge. With `Limit at the swing` they keep extending until a later candle trades the entry price. That candle is included and the lines stop there. `Market at close` leaves that edge on the signal candles. The candle that draws them does not count as that touch. A candle that trades the stop hides that whole mark, including a stop traded after the entry. The drawing candle does not count as the stop touch. With `Limit at the swing`, the stop starts on the previous candle, at that candle's low for a bullish signal and its high for a bearish signal. With `Market at close`, the stop is the open of the displacement candle and the entry is that candle's close. The stop is drawn even when the target is off. With `Limit at the swing`, the entry starts on the signal candle, on the same bars as the stop, at the swing the signal closed through. A close through a marked candle draws the entry when that candle closes through the current swing. With no swing beyond that close, the stop and the target stay on the signal candle. The green line is the target price and starts on the signal candle. A target timeframe below the chart leaves the swing target off. The displacement candle still highlights.

`Latest` keeps the newest displacement candle. `All` keeps every retained formation, up to 100. When that store is full, the oldest formation whose candle and gap have both been touched is removed. If none has been touched, the new formation is skipped.

## Liquidity

`Liquidity Timeframe` defaults to 4H. The choices are Off, Same, 1M, 5M, 15M, 1H, 4H, and Daily. `Same` uses `Timeframe Target`. The pool is the last closed candle on that timeframe. A chart candle sweeps it when its wick trades beyond one side and the close finishes back inside that candle. A bullish sweep trades below the candle's low and closes back between that low and that high. A bearish sweep trades above the candle's high and closes back between that high and that low. A later candle that closes through the swept side invalidates that sweep before the mark. A bullish sweep dies on a close below the swept low. A bearish sweep dies on a close above the swept high. A wick through that side does not. The next sweep of that side starts a new valid event. A continuation follows a close through a sweep. The candle that closed through is the breaching candle. A later candle sweeps that breaching candle, and a later candle closes through that sweep. The entry is the close of that last candle. The stop is its open. The target and `Min RR` use that distance. The box is that candle. With liquidity on, this mark also closes an open trade. The strategy sends a market order on that candle. The fill is that close. The stop is that candle's open, and the stop order is sent on the candle after it. `VWAP Chase` defaults to off. It skips a continuation whose close is farther than `VWAP Chase R` times its own stop distance past the session VWAP. Uncheck it to keep every continuation. Swing breaks and flips are not filtered. A later swing break the other way, after a mark has confirmed, is also a continuation. A bullish close through a swing high after a short, or a bearish close through a swing low after a long, is that bar. The entry is that candle's close and the stop is its open. An open trade in the other direction is reversed into this continuation. The fresh fair value gap does not also take that bar. `Key Level` defaults to Daily and uses the same choices. It does not have to be wicked on the same bars as the liquidity sweep. An untapped high or low of that candle starts the watch when a chart candle sweeps it or closes through it. A later chart candle then has to close back inside that key candle's high and low. `Liquidity Lookback` counts from that close. The liquidity sweep can be older, and it stays valid until a later close through the swept side. With `Key Level` set and `Liquidity Timeframe` off, nothing is marked. A timeframe below the chart leaves the filter out.

`Liquidity Lookback` defaults to 1 chart candle. The liquidity sweep on the signal candle counts, and so does a sweep on the candle before it. 0 allows only a sweep on the signal candle. On a 15-minute chart, 1 is 15 minutes. With `Key Level` on, that count starts at the chart candle that closed back inside the key range. The liquidity sweep is not required to fall inside it. With `Key Level` off, a bullish displacement needs a bullish sweep inside that lookback, and a bearish displacement needs a bearish sweep. Outside it, the break is still booked and the candle stays unmarked. With `Key Level` on, the displacement still needs a valid liquidity sweep, and the lookback applies to the close back inside the key range. When a mark appears, a dashed line runs from the first chart candle that swept that higher-timeframe candle to the mark, and a label sits on the line. Later taps of the same candle do not add another line. A sweep that never becomes a mark stays unmarked. The text follows the Liquidity choice, such as `4H Sweep` when that choice is 4H. With Key Level on, the same kind of line is drawn for the key candle. Its text follows the Key Level choice, such as `Daily Sweep`. A bullish line uses the swept low. A bearish line uses the swept high. The line is removed when that box is removed. A continuation draws its line from the candle that swept the breaching candle. Up to 50 of those lines stay on the chart. A flip is considered only when its direction passes that same check. Outside it, the working mark stays.

## Time window

`Use Time Window` defaults on. The default clock is 9:00 inclusive until 16:00 exclusive in `America/New_York`. The window is half-open in the selected timezone: the start minute is included and the end minute is excluded. Equal start and end values leave the window open all day. An end earlier than the start wraps past midnight.

`Minute Window` applies only while `Use Time Window` is on. It is the minute inside each hour, in that same timezone. The start minute is included and the end minute is excluded. The default is 1 to 55, so a 15-minute chart keeps :15, :30, and :45 and drops the :00 candle. 0 to 15 marks 9:00 inclusive until 9:15 exclusive when the hour is 9. The end minute has to be later than the start minute. With the switch off, the clock and the minute window both stay out of the way.

`Skip Sundays` defaults off. Turn it on and a Sunday candle does not arm a displacement, does not book a swing, and does not flip a mark. A break whose confirming candle falls on Sunday is cleared and left undrawn. An open trade keeps its stop and target. A working limit can still fill or cancel on Sunday, because the market is open. Higher-timeframe swings still include Sunday prices.

A close through a swing outside the time window is booked when `Swing Breach Lookback` expires. It does not draw a formation. With the lookback at 0, that booking happens on the break candle. A wick that fails to close through does not update the swing or the direction.

A flip has to sit inside the window. A swing break has to sit inside the window on the candle that closes through the swing. With `Limit at the swing`, the confirming candle also has to sit inside the window. With `Market at close`, that confirming candle can fall outside it, because the fill is the break candle's close. A continuation has to sit inside the window on the close-through candle. Outside the window, a flip removes the old mark and does not draw a new one, and the strategy does not send the order.

## Entry

`Limit at the swing` puts the black line on the breached swing. The strategy rests a limit at that price when the mark is drawn. The candle that draws the mark does not fill that limit.

`Entry Model` defaults to `Limit at the swing`. `Both` keeps that limit and also draws the market entry. The market entry is the displacement close and its stop is that candle's open. The swing entry stays on the breached swing, with the previous candle as its stop. Each leg keeps its own Min RR. A leg that misses it is left off. The market lines can show when the confirming candle is outside the window. The swing lines are added only when that candle is inside the window. On the strategy, Both sends the market order and rests the swing limit. Each order uses Contracts, so a mark where both fill is two times that size. The market order keeps the ids `1C Long` and `1C Short`. The swing limit uses `1C Long Limit` and `1C Short Limit`. Pyramiding is 2. Limit only and Market only still send one order.

`Market at close` puts the black line on the close of the displacement candle. For a swing break, that is the candle that closed through the swing. The next candle confirms that the swing sits inside the gap, and that candle does not set the price. For a flip, the displacement candle is the flip candle. The stop is the open of that same candle. The target and `Min RR` are measured from that close to that open, including the two-times fallback and the `Max Target` cap. When that close does not clear `Min RR`, or the stop is on the wrong side of the close, the mark is not drawn and the strategy does not send an order. The lines stay on the signal candles. A later stop still hides the mark.

The strategy sends that market order on the displacement candle, and the fill is that close. The stop price is the open of that same candle. The stop order is sent on the candle after it, once that candle has left the swing inside the gap. When the gap does not confirm, the strategy closes the fill on that candle. The script processes orders on the bar close. A limit is sent on the candle after the mark, so the mark's candle does not fill it. An existing strategy on the chart can keep an older Properties setting. In Propiedades, order execution has to fill on the bar close, or the arrow stays one bar late.

## Strategy

`Single_Candle_OB_MSS_FVG_Strategy.pine` uses the same marks. While the liquidity filter is on, only a mark inside the lookback can send an order. When a mark is drawn and the account is flat, it sends the selected entry for the contract size, default 1. `Limit at the swing` rests a limit at the breached swing. `Market at close` sends a market order on the displacement candle. The fill is that close. The drawn entry is the displacement close. The stop is that candle's open. The stop and the target on that mark are the exit. While `Liquidity Timeframe` is on, a later mark closes the open trade. That mark is the sweep validated by the lower-timeframe formation. The sweep candle itself leaves the trade open. The new mark does not send an order on that bar. The close fills on that bar's close. With `Liquidity Timeframe` off, the open trade keeps its stop and target. `Min RR` above 0 sends that order only when the mark has a target. A new mark while flat replaces the working order. A flip below `Min RR` removes the old mark and, while flat, cancels the working order when that order belongs to the flipped mark. It does not place a new order. A flip outside the liquidity lookback is not considered, so that working mark and its order stay. A later candle that trades the stop hides the mark. When that happens before a limit fill, and the account is flat, it also cancels the working limit. A market order is left to fill. A stop traded after the entry hides the mark and leaves the open trade to its own stop and target. The candle that draws the mark does not fill the limit. An open trade is left to its own stop and target.
